# Touch Grass SIM

Welcome to Touch Grass SIM(README Please!)

Are you that one guy in your friend group who is a nonstop computer programmer, gamer grinder, or a hardcore doomscroller? Well, I just fixed your habits, and turned it into an idea called:

Touch Grass SIM :)

- Touch Grass SIM slowly breaks bad screen habits, by first calming their minds with grass, then slowly dimming your screen so you won't be able to scroll or do whatever, then after 5 minutes, it lets you turn it back on.

Touch Grass SIM takes YOUR permission to allow tracking, which tracks your doomscrolling habits, and either increases or decreases the timer, until the app ACTUALLY encourages you to go outside. I used it, and I find it very helpful!
------------------------------------------------

To Install Touch Grass SIM:

Go to Releases, then click the file based on your OS(.deb, .dmg, .exe)
